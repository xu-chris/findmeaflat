# Fermi Check — 2-minute version

Distilled from Cohen's `asb-problem` skill. **His version is a workshop: one criterion per exchange, evidence classes, phased niche exploration.** That is the deep pass in `grill-and-bet`, for ideas about to become bets. This one is a filter, and its value is speed.

## Scoring

Powers of ten, or the coarse fixed values below. Pick the magnitude whose neighbours are *absurd*. Do not deliberate — deliberating means the answer is "I don't know", itself a weakest link.

| # | Criterion | Asks | Scale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Plausible** | Enough people have this problem? | market size, `1k` → `1B` |
| 2 | **Self-aware** | They know it and care? | `0.01` few agree → `1.0` near-universal |
| 3 | **Lucrative** | Budget allocated? | annual net revenue per customer, `0.01` → `1M+` |
| 4 | **Liquid** | Able and willing to buy *now*? | `0.01` years between decisions → `1.0` always in market |
| 5 | **Eager (identity)** | They want to buy from *you*? | `0` structurally barred → `1.0` mission-aligned preference |
| 6 | **Eager (comparative)** | Differentiated enough to win? | `0.1` undifferentiated → `1.0` one-of-a-kind |
| 7 | **Enduring** | Still paying in a year? | `0.01` one-off → `1.0` strong lock-in |

## Verdict

```text
product of all seven ÷ 625,000
```

- **≥ 1** — viable for an indie product
- **≥ 2** — has scale-up headroom
- **well below 1** — not viable *as currently framed*

## Output — exactly two lines

```text
Fermi: 0.4 — below viable as framed.
Weakest link: Liquid (0.01) — people hunt for a flat for a few weeks every few years, so the in-market window is narrow and unpredictable.
```

**Name the weakest link, not remedies.** A weak link is what a re-frame would have to move. Moving it is shaping, and shaping is not capture.

A low score means the *framing* is wrong at least as often as the idea. Hence a weakest link rather than a verdict to discard.
